# Product Specification & Team Operations - CampusCare Hub

## 1. Product Vision
CampusCare Hub is a web platform built to relieve university clinic congestion through a structured **Three-Tier Healthcare Strategy**:
* **Tier 1 (Self-Care & Automated Support)**: Symptom checking, over-the-counter (OTC) medication lookup, and regional campus health trend mapping.
* **Tier 2 (Nurse Consultations)**: Scheduled 15-minute physical or online (Microsoft Teams) appointments, complete with student feedback ratings.
* **Tier 3 (Clinical Escalation & Administration)**: Nurse shift schedule management, progress tracking, tier advancement, and aggregate system analytics reporting.

## 2. User Roles & Access Rights
| Role | Access Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Student** | Self-registers, logs symptoms, views OTC medications, views trend maps, books consultations, and submits ratings. |
| **Nurse** | Views clinic schedule, sets availability, conducts consultations, updates patient progress, and escalates students to Tier 2/3. |
| **Admin** | Manages staff accounts, oversees clinic metrics, approves online links, and generates operational reports. |
