# Requirements: Health Trend Mapping (A400)

## 1. User Stories
* **US3.1 (View Trend Dashboard - A400)**: As a student, nurse, or administrator, I want to view aggregated campus symptom statistics and hotspot locations so I can stay informed about health trends.

## 2. Acceptance Criteria & Validation Rules
* Must aggregate data from historical symptom logs or facility distribution figures without exposing individual student identities (POPIA/privacy compliant).
* Displays total counts per symptom type and facility area.
